## Introduction

Two learners carry identical bags of maize up the same stairs. They do the same work, but the one who runs up does it faster: she is more **powerful**. Power tells us how quickly energy is transferred, whether by a person, a borehole pump or a power station.

## Key definitions

:::definition{term="Power"}
The work done per unit time, or the energy transferred per unit time.
:::

:::formula{name="Power"}
$$
P = \frac{W}{t} = \frac{\Delta E}{t}
$$

The unit is the watt: $1\ \text{W} = 1\ \text{J/s}$. Also $1\ \text{kW} = 1000\ \text{W}$ and $1\ \text{MW} = 10^{6}\ \text{W}$.
:::

## Calculating power

:::worked-solution[Running up the stairs]
A $50\ \text{kg}$ learner climbs a staircase $6.0\ \text{m}$ high in $8.0\ \text{s}$. Take $g = 9.8\ \text{N/kg}$.

1. Work done against gravity $= mgh = 50 \times 9.8 \times 6.0 = 2940\ \text{J}$.
2. Power $= \dfrac{W}{t} = \dfrac{2940}{8.0} = 367.5 \approx 370\ \text{W}$.
:::

:::worked-solution[A motor lifting a load]
1. An electric motor lifts a $200\ \text{N}$ load through $3.0\ \text{m}$ in $4.0\ \text{s}$.
2. Work $= 200 \times 3.0 = 600\ \text{J}$.
3. Useful power output $= \dfrac{600}{4.0} = 150\ \text{W}$.
:::

### Power and speed (ZIMSEC)

When a force $F$ moves an object at a steady speed $v$, it moves a distance $vt$ in time $t$, so the work done is $Fvt$ and the power is

$$
P = Fv
$$

:::worked-solution[A car on the open road]
1. A car moves at a steady $25\ \text{m/s}$ with a driving force of $1800\ \text{N}$.
2. $P = Fv = 1800 \times 25 = 45\,000\ \text{W} = 45\ \text{kW}$.
:::

## Measuring your own power

Measure your mass on bathroom scales (and find your weight), the vertical height of a flight of stairs with a tape measure, and the time to run up it with a stop-watch. Then power = weight × height ÷ time.

## Common mistakes

:::mistake{title="Power and energy are the same"}
Energy is how much is transferred (J); power is how fast (W = J/s). A $2000\ \text{W}$ kettle transfers $2000\ \text{J}$ every second.
:::

:::mistake{title="Time in minutes"}
For watts, the time must be in seconds. Convert minutes by multiplying by 60.
:::

## Exam tips

:::exam-tip
In a power experiment, list the three measurements (weight or mass, vertical height, time) and the instruments used, and give the formula.
:::

:::exam-tip
If a question gives energy and time, use $P = E/t$; if it gives force and speed (ZIMSEC), use $P = Fv$.
:::

## Summary

:::summary
- Power is the rate of doing work or transferring energy: $P = W/t = \Delta E/t$.
- The unit is the watt: $1\ \text{W} = 1\ \text{J/s}$.
- Doing the same work in less time needs more power.
- At steady speed, $P = Fv$ (ZIMSEC).
- Personal power: weight × height climbed ÷ time taken.
:::
