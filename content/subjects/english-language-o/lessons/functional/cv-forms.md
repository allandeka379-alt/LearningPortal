## Introduction

When you finish O-Level you may apply for a place at a polytechnic, a nursing school, a learnership with a company or a holiday job. Almost every application asks for a **curriculum vitae** (CV) and a **form**. ZIMSEC includes both in its functional writing: you may be asked to compile a CV from details given, or to fill in a form correctly.

Here the marks reward **accuracy** and **following instructions** more than imaginative language. A CV that is neat, complete and honest, and a form completed exactly as instructed, make a strong first impression.

## Key definitions

:::definition{term="Curriculum vitae (CV)"}
A short, organised summary of a person's details, education, skills, experience and referees, used when applying for a job or a course. (Latin: "the course of life".)
:::

:::definition{term="Referee"}
A responsible adult who knows you well (a teacher, a head, an employer, a church leader) and agrees to describe your character and abilities. Relatives are not used as referees.
:::

:::definition{term="Block capitals"}
Separate capital letters, LIKE THIS, often required on forms so that handwriting is easy to read.
:::

## The CV layout

A school leaver's CV should fit on **one page**, under clear headings, in this order:

1. **Name** as the title (not the words *Curriculum Vitae* in large letters, though some schools accept both).
2. **Personal details:** address, phone, email, date of birth, nationality (optional).
3. **Profile or career objective:** two lines about who you are and what you want.
4. **Education:** most recent first, with dates and results (or expected results).
5. **Skills:** languages, computer skills, practical skills.
6. **Experience:** jobs, volunteering, responsibilities, most recent first.
7. **Achievements and interests:** prizes, posts held, clubs.
8. **Referees:** usually two, with title, position and contact details.

## Annotated model CV

:::example{title="Model CV: a school leaver from Kwekwe"}
**NYASHA FARAI MAPFUMO** **[Name as heading]**

**Personal details** **[Facts only]**\
Address: 27 Mbizo 4, Kwekwe\
Phone: 077 000 0000 *(example number)*\
Email: nyasha.mapfumo@example.com\
Date of birth: 14 June 2009

**Profile** **[Two lines; specific]**\
Hard-working school leaver with strong results in Mathematics and Accounts, seeking a learnership in bookkeeping.

**Education** **[Most recent first, with dates]**\
2022 to 2025: Kwekwe High School. ZIMSEC O-Level: Mathematics A, Accounts A, English Language B, Combined Science B, Commerce B, Shona B, Geography C.\
2015 to 2021: Mbizo Primary School.

**Skills** **[Grouped and concrete]**\
Languages: English and Shona (fluent), Ndebele (conversational)\
Computer: word processing, spreadsheets, email

**Experience** **[Responsibilities, not vague claims]**\
December 2024 to January 2025: Shop assistant (holiday), Mapfumo General Dealer, Mbizo. Served customers, recorded daily sales in a cash book, checked stock deliveries.\
2024: Treasurer, Commerce Club. Kept records of subscriptions and presented the termly statement.

**Achievements and interests**\
Form 4 prize for Accounts (2025); school chess team; choir.

**Referees** **[Two non-relatives with contacts]**\
Mrs R. Chikowore, Accounts teacher, Kwekwe High School, 055 000 0000\
Pastor J. Ndlovu, Mbizo Community Church, 078 000 0000
:::

### CV style

- **Note form is acceptable**, but be consistent (*Served customers*, *Recorded sales*).
- **Be honest and specific:** *Recorded daily sales in a cash book* says more than *I am good with money*.
- **No photograph, no long paragraphs, no slang email addresses.**
- **Neat layout:** same format for every date and heading.

:::example{title="Useful CV phrases and action verbs"}
- Profile: *Reliable school leaver seeking…*, *Motivated learner with an interest in…*
- Experience: *Served…*, *Recorded…*, *Organised…*, *Supervised…*, *Assisted with…*
- Skills: *Fluent in…*, *Able to use…*, *Experienced in…*
- Referees: *Available on request* is not enough in an exam: give names and contacts.
:::

## Completing forms

Forms test whether you can **read and follow instructions**. Before writing anything:

1. Read the whole form once.
2. Note special instructions: *BLOCK CAPITALS*, *black ink*, *tick one box*, *delete where not applicable*, *do not write in this space*.
3. Use the format asked for dates (*DD/MM/YYYY*).
4. Answer every required section; write *N/A* (not applicable) where a section does not apply to you.
5. Sign and date where required.
6. Check spelling, especially names and email addresses.

:::example{title="Part of a completed form (registration for a holiday computer course)"}
Instructions: *Complete in BLOCK CAPITALS. Tick ONE box. Delete as appropriate.*

SURNAME: **MAPFUMO**\
FIRST NAMES: **NYASHA FARAI**\
DATE OF BIRTH (DD/MM/YYYY): **14/06/2009**\
GENDER: Male / ~~Female~~ **[Delete as appropriate: the option that does not apply is crossed out]**\
COURSE: ☐ Beginners ☑ Intermediate ☐ Advanced **[One box ticked]**\
PREVIOUS COMPUTER TRAINING: **N/A** **[No training, so N/A rather than blank]**\
In not more than 20 words, say why you want to attend: **I want to improve my spreadsheet skills for a bookkeeping learnership next year.** **[Within the word limit; a full sentence]**
:::

## Common mistakes

:::mistake{title="Ignoring BLOCK CAPITALS"}
Writing in ordinary handwriting when capitals are required is the most common error on forms.
:::

:::mistake{title="Leaving boxes blank"}
A blank box looks like an oversight. Write *N/A* if a section does not apply.
:::

:::mistake{title="Using relatives as referees"}
Referees must be independent: teachers, employers or community leaders.
:::

:::mistake{title="Exceeding word limits on forms"}
If the form says *not more than 20 words*, count them.
:::

## Exam tips

:::exam-tip
When asked to compile a CV from given details, use every detail, put each under the correct heading and order education and experience with the most recent first.
:::

:::exam-tip
In form questions, underline every instruction before you start, then tick them off as you check.
:::

## Checklist

- CV: name heading; personal details; profile; education and experience (most recent first); skills; achievements; two referees.
- CV: one page, consistent layout, specific and honest.
- Form: instructions read and followed (capitals, ticks, deletions, date format).
- Form: every section completed or marked N/A; word limits kept; signed and dated.

## Summary

:::summary
A CV is a neat one-page summary under headings (personal details, profile, education, skills, experience, achievements, referees), with the most recent items first and specific, honest details. Completing a form means reading every instruction first, then following it exactly: block capitals, ticks, deletions, date formats, N/A for sections that do not apply, word limits and a signature.
:::
